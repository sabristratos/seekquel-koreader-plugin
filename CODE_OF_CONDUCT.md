# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small plugin for reading books, not a place worth being
unpleasant in.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about e-reader firmware, not about people.
- Be patient with bug reports. Somebody describing a problem badly is still doing you a favour.
- Criticise the code, never the person who wrote it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning remarks of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else the project is
represented.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **support@seekquel.app**. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block
accounts that keep breaking them.
